Our Story

The House of NIDIVA Couture

Founded by the Lalwani family in 1985, NIDIVA Couture began as a small suitor shop in Punjab with a single promise — every suit that leaves our door must be stitched as if it were for our own family.

Four decades later, that promise still holds. Our master karigars hand-finish each piece using traditional techniques — hand-embroidered butis, hand-rolled dupattas, and chikankari work passed down through generations.

Hand-Finished Every piece inspected by our senior karigars before dispatch.
Custom Stitching Free alterations on sizes XS to 6XL for the perfect drape.
Pan-India Shipping Insured delivery to every corner of India within 5-7 days.
Pure Promise 100% authentic fabrics — guaranteed or your money back.
